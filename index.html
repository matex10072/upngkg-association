Welcome to KGSA

KGSA brings together continuing and incoming students from Kundiawa Gembogl studying at the University of Papua New Guinea. This website is a central place to find association information, student-support programs, events, and ways to connect.

Featured Programs

Academic Support

Peer encouragement, study guidance, and orientation support for students adjusting to university life.

Student Welfare

Welfare information and practical support for members during periods of need.

Culture & Community

Activities that celebrate Chimbu heritage and build leadership, service, and connection.
